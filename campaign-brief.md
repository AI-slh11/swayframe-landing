# Swayframe Meta campaign brief

## Offer

- Free trial available; duration is not yet specified.
- Access is $299 as a one-time purchase.
- Claude is separate. The free Claude plan has usage limits; a paid Claude plan is not included.

## Audience

US-based business teams that create social content repeatedly: marketing leads, content teams, social media managers, creative teams, and small agencies. Lead with the ongoing production workload and qualify applicants by company, role, team size, and current workflow.

## Draft ad

**Primary text**

Every campaign starts with an idea. Turning it into polished carousels and motion graphics takes time your team could spend elsewhere. Swayframe helps business teams shape visual stories and create original carousels and motion graphics from a brief or source material. Apply for a free trial. Access is a $299 one-time purchase; Claude is separate, and its free plan has usage limits.

**Headline**

Give your next idea a visual story

**Description**

AI creative agent for business teams. Free trial available.

**CTA**

Apply now

## Image prompt

Create a premium, photorealistic B2B software campaign image in a vertical 4:5 composition for a Meta feed ad. Show a small marketing team in a modern, credible workplace reviewing a set of polished visual content on a large screen: a coordinated multi-panel carousel and a motion-graphic storyboard. Suggest that company source material has become designed visual content through a subtle visual transition from plain notes on one side to finished creative on the other. Use a refined deep navy, warm white, and vivid teal palette with natural light and crisp detail. Keep the people and screens realistic and professional. Leave clear negative space across the top for a headline and at the bottom for a call to action. Do not include readable text, logos, fake interface labels, watermarks, robots, or exaggerated futuristic effects; the ad copy and product branding will be added separately.

## Application qualification

Ask for work email, company, role, team size, preferred output (carousel, motion graphic, or both), and how the team handles this work today. Keep a clear consent checkbox for follow-up about the trial application.

## Setup notes

- Recommended campaign objective: Leads, with the website as the conversion location after the application destination is live.
- Set a $300 lifetime budget across a 14-day initial flight (about $21.43/day average) so the account balance is capped at the user's stated total.
- The existing Meta draft has no Page selected and is marked under Financial Products and Services. The account currently returns no Page in its selector.
- The campaign should not be activated until a Page is available, the website form delivers applications, and the destination URL is live.
- No Meta Pixel or conversion API has been configured for this site. Without conversion measurement, Meta cannot optimize for submitted or qualified applications.
