What the application asks for. Your name, work email, company, role, team size, preferred creative output, and a short description of your current workflow.

Why we ask. Swayframe uses these details to review your request for trial access and respond about that application. The form includes a consent checkbox for that contact.

How the form works. When configured, submitting the form opens a prefilled message in your email app. The page does not save application answers to a Swayframe database. Your details are sent only if you choose to send the email. Your email provider may process the message as part of its service.

Cookies and ad tracking. This landing page does not currently add an analytics or Meta advertising pixel.

Questions or data requests. Contact Swayframe at the application email shown on the trial application page. The email destination must be configured before applications are accepted.
